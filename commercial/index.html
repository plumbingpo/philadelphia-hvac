Restaurant Services

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms must stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units must replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Philadelphia HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, correct tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Many restaurant comfort complaints trace back to air balance. When a kitchen runs negative, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, drags hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and leaves staff miserable at peak service. We balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

We work around your business, not the other way around. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and disruption stays minimal so the kitchen keeps running and the dining room stays comfortable.

Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and expensive water damage from clogged condensate lines. Those are real risks for any commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Our commercial installations and inspections cover heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

Consistent professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and following through on our recommendations helps keep minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or auditor asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Emergency Service

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Philadelphia

An HVAC emergency on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, empty a retail floor on a summer afternoon, overheat a server room, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Philadelphia HVAC provides fast, emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Philadelphia and surrounding areas. When you call our commercial line, we dispatch an experienced technician quickly, because every hour without climate control costs you money.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: we handle all of it on commercial equipment. Our first priority is diagnosing the fault and getting heating or cooling back as quickly as possible. Once it's running, we set up maintenance and monitoring to prevent a repeat, and every response comes with full documentation for your facility files.
